# What Works for Us — A Guide to Life Together

Compare how you’d handle everyday life and big decisions together, from routines and intimacy to long-term plans.

Open `relationship-quiz.html` in a browser. Choose **View a fictional comparison** for a quick preview. **Restore saved answers** accepts compatible Life Together JSON exports and guides you through remaining questions. The static page runs locally and needs no account or server.

## Scoring

A shared first choice earns 100 points, an overlap in acceptable options earns 75, and no overlap earns 0. Scenario scores are averaged within each topic. Each topic is weighted by the mean of the two importance ratings. Opposing firm requirements appear beside the score. Descriptive answers, personality estimates, and How I Respond are outside the score. Personality results describe self-reported tendencies and have no clinical or population norms.

## Privacy and sharing

Responses stay in the open tab. A comparison code encodes relationship and profile answers and is readable by anyone who has it. How I Respond is included only when the sender selects it. The current sharing flow is under review for v1.0.

## Checks

Run `node check.cjs` with Node 22 or later. For a browser check, complete the quiz or restore a compatible export, review both individual views, generate and compare codes, and check the report on a narrow screen.
